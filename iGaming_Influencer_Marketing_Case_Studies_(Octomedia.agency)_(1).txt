Case Study 8 — Bluechip.io (Hybrid Influencer+Ads Launch)
Case 8 overview: Bluechip.io launched globally via a mix of Twitch influencers and programmatic ads.
* Budget: $250,000
* Influencers: 45 Twitch/Kick streamers (global reach)
* Registrations: 12,000
* FTDs: 3,500
* CPA (FTD): ~$71
Strategy: Bluechip.io combined 3-week live-stream marathons (each influencer ran 10+ streams) with in-stream chatbots providing promo codes.
Results: This strategy drove ~12K regs and 3.5K FTDs. The blended CPA (~$71) was competitive – for context, a large size streamer test achieved ~€38 CPA, so the integrated campaign was efficient at scale. Registrations converted at ~29.2%, on par with high-performing iGaming influencer funnels. Bluechip.io retained 19 influencers long-term due to consistent performance.
